How SBA 7(a) loans are structured

The 7(a) program is the SBA's main small business loan. A bank or other approved lender makes the loan, and the SBA guarantees a portion of it, which lets the lender offer longer terms than a conventional loan. The maximum loan amount is $5 million.

Terms depend on what the money is for. Working capital and equipment loans commonly run up to 10 years. Loans that finance real estate can run up to 25 years. Longer terms mean lower payments, which is often the main reason a business chooses SBA financing over a conventional term loan.

Rates

Most 7(a) loans carry a variable rate set at the prime rate plus a spread, and the SBA caps how large that spread can be. Because the rate floats, your payment can change when prime moves. Run the calculator at your quoted rate, then again one or two points higher, to see how much room your cash flow has if rates rise.

The guarantee fee

The SBA charges the lender a guarantee fee on the guaranteed portion of the loan, and the lender usually passes it to the borrower. The fee percentage depends on loan size and maturity, and the SBA resets it each fiscal year, so check the current schedule or ask your lender for the exact figure. Many borrowers finance the fee into the loan rather than paying it at closing. Enter the fee as a percentage of the loan amount above to see its effect on the payment.

What else to expect

  • Personal guarantee: owners of 20% or more generally guarantee the loan.
  • Collateral: lenders take available business assets, and sometimes personal real estate, for larger loans.
  • Equity injection: for acquisitions and startups, expect to put in some of your own cash.
  • Prepayment fee: loans of 15 years or longer can carry a fee if paid off early in the first three years.
  • Paperwork: tax returns, interim financials, a debt schedule and a personal financial statement.

Will you qualify?

SBA lenders focus on repayment ability. Take the monthly payment above, multiply by 12, add your other debt payments, and test the total in the DSCR calculator. Most lenders look for 1.15x to 1.25x or better.

Frequently asked questions

What is the current SBA 7(a) interest rate?

Most 7(a) loans use the prime rate plus a spread capped by the SBA, so the rate changes when prime changes. Ask lenders for current quotes and enter the rate above.

How long is an SBA 7(a) loan term?

Up to 10 years for working capital and equipment is typical, and up to 25 years when the loan finances real estate.

Can the SBA guarantee fee be financed?

Usually, yes. Many borrowers add the fee to the loan amount. Enter the fee percentage above to see the effect on your payment.

What is the maximum SBA 7(a) loan?

The program maximum is $5 million.
