About Credit Desk Tools

Credit Desk Tools makes free finance calculators and ready-to-use Excel templates for small business owners, bookkeepers and the people who lend to them.

The tools are built by a commercial lending and accounting professional with years of experience underwriting purchase order financing, asset-based loans and working capital deals. Every calculator follows the same math lenders use to size and price loans, and every page shows its formula so you can check it yourself.

What we believe

  • Know your numbers first. Owners who understand their DSCR, cash cycle and true cost of capital get better terms and avoid expensive mistakes.
  • Show the math. No black boxes. If a number matters, you should be able to see how it was calculated.
  • Plain English. Finance has enough jargon. We explain what each number means and what to do about it.

Templates

For deeper analysis, our Excel and Google Sheets templates cover loan readiness, 13-week cash flow forecasting, borrowing base certificates, financial statement spreading and more. Browse the templates.
