Why these calculators are different

Most finance calculators stop at a payment amount. These are built by people who underwrite business loans and close the books, so they answer the questions a lender will ask: whether your cash flow covers the debt, what a factoring or merchant cash advance quote really costs per year, and how long your cash is tied up in inventory and receivables.

Each page explains the formula in plain English, so you can check the math and use it in your own planning.
